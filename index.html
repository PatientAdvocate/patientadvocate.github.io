Welcome to Patient Advocate

Patient Advocate (PA) helps patients and caregivers quickly and safely find clinical trial options tailored to their specific needs. By securely reading patient clinical data, PA analyzes each patient's unique history and matches them with relevant clinical trials. PA simplifies the search for potentially life-changing treatments, giving patients access to new therapies and research options that align with their health status. We aim to empower patients and their loved ones to make more informed choices on their care.
